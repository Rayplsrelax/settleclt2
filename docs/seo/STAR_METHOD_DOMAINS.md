# STAR Method: SEO Domain Strategy for Settle CLT

The STAR method identifies which keyword domains Settle CLT can realistically compete in and win.

---

## Framework

**S** — Specificity (how niche is the keyword?)
**T** — Trust signals (does our site have authority here?)
**A** — Actionability (can we create content that satisfies intent?)
**R** — Reachability (can we rank on page 1 within 6 months?)

---

## Domain Analysis

### Domain 1: Charlotte Neighborhood Guides

| Factor | Score | Reasoning |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Specificity | 9/10 | Hyper-local, long-tail keywords |
| Trust | 8/10 | 20 detailed guides, local data, resident perspectives |
| Actionability | 10/10 | We have the content infrastructure |
| Reachability | 8/10 | Low competition for specific neighborhoods |

**Target keywords:**
- `[neighborhood] charlotte nc` (e.g., "dilworth charlotte nc")
- `living in [neighborhood] charlotte`
- `[neighborhood] neighborhood guide`
- `moving to [neighborhood]`
- `[neighborhood] apartments`
- `[neighborhood] restaurants`

**Content strategy:**
- longDescription on each neighborhood page (500-1000 words)
- Blog posts diving deeper into specific neighborhoods
- Comparison content (e.g., "South End vs NoDa")
- "Moving to [neighborhood]" guides

**Current status:** Active. South Charlotte and Dilworth have long-form content. Expand to all 20.

## Priority Ranking

1. **Charlotte Neighborhood Guides** — Highest STAR score, lowest competition, most content ready

3. **Charlotte Events** — High search volume, needs weekly content cadence
